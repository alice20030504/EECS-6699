# EECS-6699 Final Project Guidelines

## Paper Outline (~15 pages)

### 1. Introduction
- Describe the general problem area, DL and specific subtopic(s)
- Brief literature review

### 2. Detailed Problem Description
- More detailed literature review for selected problem(s)
- Detailed description of known results (theoretical or experimental)

### 3. Some Reproduction
- Theoretical or experimental partial/full reproduction of results
- e.g., run simulations that illustrate main results

### 4. New Results
- Describe in detail your results (theoretical or experimental)
- If experimental, describe experiments and results
- Explain clearly the graphs and tables from experiments

### 5. Discussion and Conclusion
- Draw general inferences from your results
- Compare to known results from the literature

## Key Differences & Guiding Questions

The focus should be on **NN properties** instead of applications:

- **What did you learn about a neural network?**
- **How do the changes in NN impact its performance?**
  - Changes could be: architecture (e.g., width/depth), activation function, training method, normalization, dropout...
- In class we focused on plain vanilla feed-forward networks, but you could choose other types (e.g., ResNets)

### Themes from Class

1. **Approximation and interpolation theory** and the impact of depth
2. **Optimization landscape** and global convergence
3. **Generalization theory**: conditions for small/bounded testing errors

Many problems were formulated in the context of wide/over-parametrized networks with two types of scaling: NTK/lazy training or mean-field/active training.
